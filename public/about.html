About InsurePlain

⚠️ Educational only. InsurePlain does not provide insurance, legal, or financial advice. Always consult a qualified insurance professional about your specific situation.

InsurePlain is an independent educational resource focused on explaining insurance terms, concepts, and common situations in plain English.

Insurance policies are often written in language that is difficult to understand. InsurePlain was created to help readers better understand general insurance concepts — such as deductibles, coinsurance, exclusions, and claims processes — so they can have more informed conversations with their insurance provider or a licensed professional.

What InsurePlain Is

An educational content site. We publish general information about insurance terminology and concepts based on publicly available information and common industry practices.

What InsurePlain Is Not

InsurePlain is not an insurance company, agency, broker, or advisor. We do not sell insurance products, and we are not affiliated with any insurance provider. We do not provide insurance, legal, financial, or medical advice, and nothing on this site should be interpreted as a guarantee, recommendation, or professional opinion regarding your individual situation, policy, or claim.

Accuracy of Information

Insurance terms, laws, and regulations vary by state, provider, and individual policy, and are subject to change. While we aim for accuracy, InsurePlain makes no warranties or representations regarding the completeness, reliability, or current accuracy of any content on this site. Readers should always consult their policy documents or a licensed insurance professional for guidance specific to their situation.
